### Story
Beni Moss finds three photographs of herself filed against another woman's identity. To release her own image, a clerk asks Beni to certify that Ella Price has no living claimant. A red-hat former night-run employee offers a testable detail about the original delivery envelope. When Beni refuses the declaration and Holt records that refusal, an unexplained APPROVED filing appears.

### What this experiment tests
- Filmable psychological realism and everyday bureaucracy as the horror instrument.
- Source-faithful Beni face/body/wardrobe using already-approved reference assets.
- Distinct characters with objective, cost, contradiction, and credible dialogue.
- Constrained supernatural uncertainty that cannot replace documents and testimony.
- Shared LIKENESS thematic identity without forced SECOND HAND canon.
