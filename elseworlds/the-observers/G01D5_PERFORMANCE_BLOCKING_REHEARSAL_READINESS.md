## Story logic and directing law
The institution's false certified record causes an ordinary clerk to act as though a third witness exists. The clerk must **notice** the contradiction, have the **option to correct** it, and **choose** to verify it anyway. A cup placed at an empty chair is the consequence of that choice. No ghost, chair movement, third voice, hypnosis, or visual anomaly.

## Ground plan — coordinate authority candidate
Rectangular annex, 6 m wide (west-east) × 4.5 m deep (south-north); dimensions are blocking **proposals**, not measured production architecture.
- South wall: chairs C1, C2, C3 in a straight row, C3 blue and visibly empty. C1/C2 occupied by W1/W2.
- East side: clerk desk facing west, with sightline to all three chairs.
- West wall: public entrance, no extras crossing after opening.
- North wall: fixed security camera with full chair-row sightline.
- Clerk starts behind desk, remains there through VERIFIED, then crosses along a clear path to C3 for the cup.
- No crossing the chair-row camera axis between opening wide and ending wide. Security angle must agree with actor occupancy and blue-chair position.

## Behavioral performance notes
**Clerk:** Avoid a theatrical hesitation. The moment of choice is a small action: hand approaches correction field, stops, then moves paper under VERIFIED stamp. Make institutional risk avoidance visible, not villainy. No direct camera looks.
**Witnesses:** Waiting, impatient but ordinary; do not monitor the paperwork or react to an unseen person. No synchronized or prophetic speech.
**Cup action:** Establish cup at desk before the final crossing. It is moved by the clerk, never appears by a cut. The cup should be placed on the surface beside C3, not on the seat unless that action has clear human motivation.
**Dialogue:** Only the three short opening lines. No voiceover or exposition.
