## 3. Pilot evidence test — THE EMPTY CHAIR
**Length:** 90 seconds, paper/audio-only proof.  
**Place:** New, unestablished public records annex waiting room (neutral location).  
**Cast:** Two offscreen witnesses (temporary original voices); no Thoreau, Beni, Mimic or Teller on camera.  
**Prop:** a single blue numbered waiting-room chair, paper attendance slip, two independent recorders. Do not borrow SECOND HAND recorder A/B.  
**Rule:** Camera footage is ordinary and correct; only the certified transcript falsely includes a third witness. The contradiction must be independently testable.

### Timed beat sheet
| Time | Picture | Sound / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 00–12 | Static 16:9 waiting room. Two people are present; empty blue chair remains visible. | Fluorescent ballast, ticket printer, one cough. No score. |
| 12–25 | A clerk stamps an attendance slip for TWO. | Physical stamp and paper shuffle; recordable time-of-day tone. |
| 25–38 | Cut to 4:3 fixed security view. Blue chair is still empty. | Source room tone, correctly delayed HVAC cycle. |
| 38–52 | Certified transcript printout appears: THREE WITNESSES. | Printer ticks three times. The third name is redacted, not invented by generative typography. |
| 52–66 | Independent pocket recorder playback from the same moment. | Two voices, then an empty interval where a third voice is labeled on transcript. |
| 66–78 | Clerk compares the attendance slip to the transcript, but chooses to stamp the transcript 'VERIFIED'. | Stamp lands over the room's HVAC cutoff. |
| 78–90 | Return to 16:9; empty chair unchanged. Clerk quietly sets a third cup of water on its armrest. | A glass touching plastic. Cut before explanation. |

### Evidence chain
- E1: wide master establishes exactly two witnesses and an empty chair.
- E2: attendance slip, physically stamped for TWO before the discrepancy.
- E3: fixed CCTV independently confirms the chair remains empty.
- E4: certified transcript claims THREE and is deliberately verified.
- E5: pocket audio preserves the absence of a third voice.
- E6: third cup is a *human choice caused by institutional authority*, not proof of a ghost.

### Camera / audio proof constraints
- ALEXA 35 / Cooke S4-like 40mm 16:9 wide; no artificial push-in.
- 4:3 insert must be a separately composed locked CCTV plate, not arbitrary crop.
- 48kHz/24-bit multitrack: room, printer, stamp, voices, pocket playback.
- No glitch, face generation, horror music, gore, inexplicable objects, or unmotivated camera motion.
- Composite all readable transcript and slip text in post; no image-model text.
- Zero credits: storyboard, paper edit, synthetic silence/room-tone mockup or manually captured source only.
