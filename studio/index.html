/ˈɒmniə vaˈnitas/ — all is vanity. The mirror, the tape, the face you trust.

A studio that makes psychological rooms you cannot fully leave. The room remembers what love edits out. We build one world — the LIKENESS universe — and let it spill across a game, a tape, a wardrobe, and a score.

§ OVS-00 // THE DOCTRINE

Five rules the whole universe obeys.

01
EVIDENCE, NOT EXPLANATION

We hand you the record and let you carry the question home. The work never tells you what it meant — it repeats, delays, and withholds until the meaning is yours to accuse.

02
IDENTITY IS A LOCK

Every face, garment, and voice is pinned to a reference. The horror is drift — the moment the copy answers for the original and no one in the room corrects it.

03
THE ROOM REMEMBERS

Space is the antagonist. Objects keep contradictory versions of the truth, and your own memory might be the edited copy playing back.

04
DIEGETIC FIRST

Nothing announces itself as art. The score is room tone, the wardrobe is a real closet, and the thing in the doorway is wearing your coat.

05
RESTRAINT IS THE BUDGET

Oxblood is rationed and gore is cheap. We spend on timing, on delay, on the reflection that answers a half-second late.
