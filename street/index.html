§ EXT-02 // FIELD NOTE

A street is just a room with the walls walked away.

The brand version of this page put real people on the pavement and let the city cast them. Ours does the same — except the city keeps handing us the same two faces, dressed as everyone else. You can leave Room 14. You cannot leave the likeness. The exterior is not an escape; it is the widest shot of the same argument.
