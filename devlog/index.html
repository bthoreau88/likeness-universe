UNIVERSE

Anthology restructure + cohesion pass

Established the LIKENESS UNIVERSE as the umbrella: LIKENESS : THE GAME (Dellwood motel) as the flagship and TAPE 03 (the apartment) as a canonical tape, each tagged. Added a press fact sheet and quarantined the early sci-fi cut to a non-canon /alt page.

Data-driven routes online

Characters, Evidence, Tapes, and the Motel Map now read from the shared registry (src/lib/registry.ts), mirroring the game's CSV registries. Matching IDs across site and game; content expands without losing identity.

CANON

Recharacterized to the LIKENESS world

Stripped the generic sci-fi framing; rebuilt the front-of-site around THOREAU, DRYA, the double, the tapes, and the room that testifies. Visual DNA, color matrix, camera law, and the eight-beat architecture are all on the page.

ARCHIVE

Real captures placed

~35 in-engine frames threaded into Captured, the Apartment room tour, Identity Lock sheets, Recovered Footage, Evidence macros, the Choice closing band, and two motion clips. Authored atmosphere fills the frames still to shoot.

ENTRY

Universe landing built

A full-screen, mouse-scrub video entry (move to scrub the room), typewriter archive interface, and pill navigation — recustomized from the Mainframe interaction spec into the OVS dark cinematic system.

EXPANSION

Image-Blaster, NanoGS, Higgsfield, and the UE Guide logged as optional accelerators. Per the Expansion Law: they prototype, enrich, test, and market — they do not replace the authored story or the locked identities.
