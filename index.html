OVS // DOSSIER · TAPE-03
THE PREMISE

The room remembers what you forgot.

TAPE-03 is a single-location psychological thriller about identity, memory, and the doppelgänger you can almost trust. A man wakes with a head wound, a camcorder on the floor, and no memory of the last forty-eight hours. Three labeled tapes explain what happened. The apartment is wrong in small ways. Someone with his voice is inside.

  • WAKE3:14 AM. A wound, a camcorder, and three tapes you don't remember making.
  • RECONSTRUCTThe rooms rearrange when you look away. Evidence, not explanation.
  • CHOOSETwo endings, both ambiguous. The room keeps the half you edited out.

§ 04 // CAMERA LAW

The camera is a witness.

  • PERSPECTIVEFirst-person for traversal. Third-person for waking, tape playback, the final door. The shift from first to third is the language of dissociation.
  • LENSFOV 75 default · 65 in stealth · 90 in reality-shift. Tape footage simulates a 35mm consumer camcorder — soft, barrel-distorted, no anamorphic squeeze.
  • MOVEMENTStatic unless the story needs the room alive. Micro-movement belongs to objects — dust, breath, reflection, lamp flicker. Never a Steadicam glide.
  • THE OTHERAlways at frame edge, half-occluded, or out of focus. Never centered. Never sharp. The player must doubt that they saw what they saw.

TAPE 03THE APARTMENT · LOCATION BIBLE

The apartment has a will.

Five named rooms plus one that does not exist on the floor plan until Scene 3. The apartment is the antagonist — it rearranges when the player isn't looking, withholds, and returns objects in the wrong order. The horror is geographical, not supernatural.

§ DETECTIVE LOOP // OBJECT MACROS

Evidence. Not decoration.

Every story-critical prop, photographed as evidence — macro framing, shallow depth, fingerprints, dust, motivated light. The inserts that thread between the rooms.

OVS // IN MOTION

OVS // WHAT THE GAME IS

A chamber horror that haunts, not answers.

The room is the antagonist

No combat. No monster to fight. The apartment rearranges when you look away, withholds, and returns objects in the wrong order. The horror is geographical.

Tape as mechanic

Three VHS tapes are the only witnesses. Scrub, rewind, and cross-reference what you said before you forgot it.

No HUD. Only blackouts

Diegetic everything — the menu is a CRT, pause is an ejected tape. No restart screen, only the gaps in your memory.

Two endings. Both true

Evidence, not explanation. You finish carrying a question, not an answer — and neither ending lets the room off the hook.
